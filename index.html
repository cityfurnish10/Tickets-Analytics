⚡ Why Anjali Failed — Every Unique Reason, Fully Explained
1No Service Request access — couldn't create, read, or update SRs9 tickets · 23%▶
Customer Needed

To have a service request logged for a product issue, repair, or complaint — and get an SR number to track progress.

Anjali Couldn't

Zero read/write access to Zoho Desk. She could not open a ticket, look up an existing one, or confirm if an SR had been raised.

Human Did

Manually opened Zoho Desk, raised the SR, shared the number with the customer — work that should be fully automated.

The Fix

Zoho Desk API: POST /tickets (create SR), GET /tickets/{id} (read status), PATCH /tickets/{id} (update). Anjali logs every complaint as an SR automatically and shares the number in-chat.

2No real-time logistics access — delivery / technician ETA unknown13 tickets · 33%▶
Customer Needed

A specific delivery or technician arrival window — not a generic promise that "someone will reach out."

Anjali Couldn't

No access to the logistics or field service platform. She defaulted to vague assurances — the exact thing the customer was already frustrated about.

Human Did

Opened the logistics dashboard, found the actual ETA or technician assignment, and gave the customer a real time window.

The Fix

Logistics Tracking API: GET /delivery/status?order={id} and GET /technician/eta?sr={id}. Anjali pulls live status on demand and gives a real window — no human required.

3No billing access — couldn't see invoices, plans, or validate coupons9 tickets · 23%▶
Customer Needed

To understand a charge, confirm their current plan, or know why a discount code wasn't applied.

Anjali Couldn't

No read access to Odoo billing. She couldn't pull invoices, check plan details, or validate coupon codes.

Human Did

Logged into Odoo, found the customer's invoice, validated the coupon or plan, and resolved the discrepancy.

The Fix

Odoo Billing API: GET /invoices?customer={id}, GET /plans/{id}, GET /coupons/validate?code={code}. Anjali pulls and explains any charge in real time.

4No Odoo account access — couldn't see subscription items or address5 tickets · 12%▶

To confirm or update their rented items list, delivery address, or subscription details.

Anjali Couldn't

Odoo Account API: GET /customers/{id}, GET /sale-orders?customer={id}, GET /subscriptions/{id}. Full read access to any active customer's account at query time.

5No scheduling access — couldn't check or change appointment slots1 ticket · 2%▶

To reschedule a repair or delivery appointment to a different date or time window.

Config-based decision ruleset: auto-approve waivers ≤₹500 for subscriptions older than 6 months; flag anything larger for human review. Anjali acts within pre-approved guardrails without escalating every case.

1No real-time logistics visibility — couldn't see delivery status32 tickets · 54%▶
Customer Needed

To know where their delivery was, when it would arrive, or why it hadn't come yet — a live status, not a guess.

Anjali Couldn't

She could see "dispatched" in the order system but had no access to the live logistics feed. She raised a generic escalation and promised a callback, which often never came.

Human Did

Opened the logistics platform, found the driver's live status or delay reason, gave the customer an actual updated ETA or rescheduled the slot.

The Fix

Real-time Logistics API: GET /delivery/status?order={id} returns current driver position, delay reason, and ETA. Anjali resolves 54% of Order Status tickets without human intervention.

2No scheduled delivery status — couldn't confirm today's window10 tickets · 17%▶

To confirm whether today's scheduled delivery was still on track — a simple ETA check before waiting home all day.

Human Did

Checked dispatch status, confirmed the driver was en-route or updated the customer if the slot had slipped.

The Fix

Same Logistics API: GET /delivery/schedule?order={id}&date=today returns dispatch confirmation and time-window. Covered by the same integration as failure #1.

KYC Status API: GET /kyc/{customer_id}/status returns current stage, blocking reason, and SLA timestamp. Anjali gives an accurate answer and escalates to verification only when genuinely blocked.

Escalation Tracker: GET /escalations?customer={id} shows open items, owner, and SLA status. POST /escalations creates a tracked item with an owner assigned. No more lost callbacks.

⚡ Why Anjali Failed — Every Unique Reason, Fully Explained
1No cart creation in chat — 168 warm leads handed off to sales168 tickets · 49%▶
Customer Needed

To complete their rental order inside the chat — they had already confirmed the product, city, and plan. They just needed someone to take the order.

Anjali Couldn't

No API access to create a cart, add items, or generate a checkout link. Every purchase-ready conversation ended with: "Let me connect you with our sales team" — breaking the conversion.

Human Did

Sales agent called back, rebuilt the conversation, created the order in Odoo manually. Many leads went cold before the callback happened.

The Fix

Cart Creation API: POST /cart/create, POST /cart/{id}/items, GET /cart/{id}/checkout-url. Anjali builds the cart in-chat and sends a payment-ready link. 49% of this category closes without a human.

2No inventory visibility — couldn't confirm stock or offer alternatives34 tickets · 10%▶
Customer Needed

To know if the specific product was available in their city — and if not, what the best alternative was or when it would be back.

Anjali Couldn't

No access to live inventory. She would say "that item may be out of stock, I'll check" and then escalate — delivering the same dead-end the customer was trying to avoid.

Human Did

Checked the inventory system, confirmed availability or found an alternative SKU in the same city, and created the cart with the alternative.

The Fix

Inventory API: GET /products/{id}/availability?city={city} (stock + ETA), GET /products/{id}/alternatives?city={city} (substitutes). Anjali confirms, offers alternatives, or adds to waitlist — no escalation needed.

3Policy knowledge gaps — wrong or missing answers on tenure, deposits, AC rules55 tickets · 16%▶
Customer Needed

Accurate, city-specific policy answers: AC minimum tenure, deposit amounts, cancellation terms, combo eligibility, maintenance schedules.

Anjali Couldn't

Either gave approximate or wrong policy info, or escalated because she wasn't trained on city-specific variations. AC tenure rules, Jaipur deposit norms, and combo eligibility were consistent blind spots.

Human Did

Looked up the correct policy for the city and product type, explained it accurately, and in most cases then also created the order that should have been created in the same session.

The Fix

Structured Policy Knowledge Base: queryable rules by city, product category, and tenure. All edge cases (AC norms, deposit rules, combo eligibility) encoded as structured facts — not embedded in training text that degrades over time.

4Coupon and promo codes not validatable — customer stuck at checkout28 tickets · 8%▶
Customer Needed

To know whether their coupon code was valid, what discount it offered, and why it wasn't applying during checkout.

Anjali Couldn't

No access to the promo/billing system to validate codes. She couldn't confirm expiry, eligibility conditions, or the discount amount — so every coupon question became an escalation.

Human Did

Looked up the coupon in Odoo, confirmed whether it was valid, explained the conditions, and in some cases applied it manually or issued a replacement code.

The Fix

Billing API: GET /coupons/validate?code={code}&customer={id} returns validity, discount type, eligibility conditions. Anjali tells the customer immediately whether the code works and why — and applies it if the cart is being built in-chat.

5No approval authority — escalated exceptions that had clear rules25 tickets · 7%▶
Customer Needed

A reasonable exception — a small discount on a B2B order, a monthly plan for a high-value subscription, or enterprise routing for a bulk inquiry.

Anjali Couldn't

Anjali understood the request correctly but had no ruleset for exceptions. Every discount or routing decision required a human, even when the logic was simple and repeatable.

Human Did

Applied a discount, approved a monthly plan, or routed the inquiry to the enterprise sales team — decisions that follow a clear pattern and could be automated.

The Fix

Config-based ruleset: auto-approve ≤5% discount on first B2B order above ₹5,000/mo; offer monthly plan for ₹4,000+/mo subscriptions; route bulk (≥10 items) to enterprise sales. Encode the decision — don't escalate it.

6Unnecessary escalations — Anjali escalated, human couldn't resolve either24 tickets · 7%▶
Customer Needed

A resolution — from anyone. The escalation didn't add value because the human agent also lacked the information or authority to resolve.

Anjali Couldn't

Anjali's confidence threshold was miscalibrated. She escalated cases she could have handled (with the APIs above) and also escalated cases where the right answer was "we'll follow up" — which a human also couldn't improve on.

Human Did

In many of these cases: nothing more than what Anjali did. The escalation added a wait time but no resolution value.

The Fix

Two fixes: (1) Give Anjali the APIs above so fewer things genuinely require escalation. (2) Tune confidence thresholds — Anjali should only escalate when a human can actually do something she cannot. If both would say "we'll follow up," Anjali should say it and close.
